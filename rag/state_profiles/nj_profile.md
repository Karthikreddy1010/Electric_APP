# New Jersey (NJ) Electricity Benchmark & Profile

## Current Rates & Trend
- **Residential Rate**: 18.67 ¢/kWh (2024 annual avg), current 2026 estimate ~19.50 ¢/kWh.
- **5-Year Growth**: Rates increased ~18.2% from 2020 to 2025.
- **National Rank**: #14 most expensive residential rate in the US.
- **Regional Rank**: #8 in the Northeast.

## Sector Comparison
- **Residential**: 18.67 ¢/kWh
- **Commercial**: 13.85 ¢/kWh
- **Industrial**: 10.42 ¢/kWh
- **Transportation**: 12.10 ¢/kWh

## Key Utility Regulators & Suppliers
- Major Electric Utilities: Public Service Electric and Gas (PSE&G), Jersey Central Power & Light (JCP&L), Atlantic City Electric (ACE), Rockland Electric (RECO).
- Rate Structure Components: Basic Generation Service (BGS) Auction supply charges, PSEG Distribution charges, Transmission charges, System Benefits Charge (SBC).
