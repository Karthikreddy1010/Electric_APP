# EIA Electricity Retail Prices Context Summary (2001–2026)

## Overview
The U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Monthly Electricity Retail Sales dataset provides state-level monthly metrics for:
- Average Retail Price (cents/kWh)
- Total Retail Sales (million kWh)
- Total Revenue (million dollars)
- Total Customer Count

## Key Statistics (2024–2026)
- **National Residential Average Price**: ~16.48 to 17.30 cents/kWh.
- **New Jersey (NJ) Residential Average Price**: ~18.67 to 19.50 cents/kWh.
- **Highest Price States**: Hawaii (~39.41¢), Massachusetts (~28.05¢), California (~27.63¢), Connecticut (~27.36¢).
- **Lowest Price States**: Washington (~10.50¢), Idaho (~10.74¢), Wyoming (~11.45¢), Utah (~11.52¢).

## Sector Breakdowns
1. **Residential (RES)**: Highest unit price, strong summer/winter seasonal peaking.
2. **Commercial (COM)**: Medium unit price, steady load.
3. **Industrial (IND)**: Lowest unit price due to high-voltage transmission delivery and high load factor.
4. **Transportation (TRA)**: EV and transit electric pricing.
