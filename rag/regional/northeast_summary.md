## Key Insights
- New Jersey has the lowest average residential rate among immediate tri-state neighbors (lower than NY and CT, though slightly higher than PA).
- Northeast electricity rates are on average 30-50% higher than the national average due to fuel delivery constraints, grid congestion, and aggressive decarbonization policies.
